Privacy Policy – Arabic Shorts Bot

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who uses this app

Arabic Shorts Bot is a private tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel to upload videos to their own channel. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the signed-in user's own YouTube channel.
  • Read-only access to public YouTube data (search results, video details) and the user's own channel name.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to find videos, create Arabic Shorts and upload them to the owner's channel.
  • The login token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Video transcripts and titles are sent to an AI translation service (Anthropic Claude) only to create the Arabic translation.

Removing access

You can remove this app's access at any time at Google Account permissions. Deleting the app's folder deletes all locally stored data.
